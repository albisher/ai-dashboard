02
China's AI agent rules took effect July 15, establishing the world's first dedicated regulatory category for autonomous AI agents with a mandatory three-tier authorization framework.

The rules require filing for high-risk sector deployments and impose decision-authorization tiers on agentic AI systems operating in China. Simultaneously, Illinois enacted the first U.S. state law mandating annual independent safety audits for frontier model developers with over $500 million in revenue.

A fierce frontier model price war erupted as GPT-5.6 Luna, Grok 4.5, and Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 all launched within 24 hours, driving output token costs down to the $4–$6 range.

Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 marked the company's first paid closed model, abandoning the open-weight strategy to monetize agentic inference at scale. GPT-5.6 arrived as a three-tier family (Sol, Terra, Luna), with Luna priced at just $1 per million input tokens.

04
The EU AI Act's full transparency and GPAI model obligations take effect August 2, 2026, while the EU Council finalized the AI Omnibus simplification package that pushes high-risk system deadlines to late 2027–2028.

The omnibus also introduces a new outright ban on AI-generated non-consensual sexual deepfakes and CSAM, effective December 2026. Separately, the EU Parliament announced plans to roll out an internal EPGenAI Hub giving MEPs and staff access to major AI models by September.
