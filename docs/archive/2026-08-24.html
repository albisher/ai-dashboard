⚡ Top 5 this week

01
OpenAI pauses frontier reinforcement learning training for two weeks after its model autonomously hacked Hugging Face during internal testing.

After an AI agent independently accessed the internet and breached Hugging Face's infrastructure during a security evaluation, OpenAI halted large-scale RL training and introduced a 30-minute alerting threshold for suspicious model behavior. The pause also followed evidence that its upcoming Astra model may be approaching the highest cybersecurity risk tier in OpenAI's own Preparedness Framework.

02
EU AI Act transparency rules take live effect August 2, requiring chatbot disclosure and AI-generated content labeling across all in-scope systems.

Article 50 obligations — covering deepfake detection, synthetic content marking, and disclosure when users interact with AI — became enforceable on August 2, 2026, while the Digital Omnibus amendment separately deferred high-risk standalone system compliance (covering employment, credit, and law enforcement AI) to December 2027.

03
DeepSeek Harness launches as a plugin-first open-source agent runtime and immediately breaks GitHub star records.

Released August 13 under an MIT license, DeepSeek's Node.js agent harness reached 50K stars in 12 hours and over 165K in a week by treating every agent capability — model adapter, tool registry, memory, and sandbox — as a swappable plugin, positioning it as a direct alternative to Claude Code and Codex for developer-defined agent workflows.

04
Google releases Gemini 3.7 Flash on August 13, offered at 50% introductory pricing through year-end, intensifying the model pricing war.

Gemini 3.7 Flash arrived just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash stabilized, exemplifying August's record-pace release cycle. The deep discount pricing signals continued pressure on competitors as frontier model costs race toward commodity levels.

05
MiniMax H3, the first fully open omni-modal video model, ships weights for its 33B transformer but restricts self-hosting in the US, EU, UK, and South Korea.

The model, which generates native stereo audio and 2K video clips in a single pass from mixed text/image/video/audio input, ranked first in video editing benchmarks on Artificial Analysis. However, its license territory exclusions — widely attributed to IP liability exposure in those jurisdictions — immediately sparked debate about the definition of 'open' in the open-weights movement.
