The new workhorse model outperformed comparable Anthropic and OpenAI offerings across nine benchmarks and is priced at $0.75 per million input tokens through end of 2026. 03
EU AI Act enforcement goes live on August 2, making GPAI transparency obligations and deepfake labeling rules legally binding across the bloc.

The European Commission's AI Office and national authorities began enforcing the Act, requiring chatbots to identify themselves, AI-generated content to carry machine-readable marks, and deepfakes to be labeled — with fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover for violations. High-risk system rules for regulated products were pushed to 2028 under the AI Omnibus amendment.

04
Meta enters the coding-agent race with Muse Code beta, a terminal agent powered by the new Muse Spark 1.2 model featuring persistent parallel sub-agents.

Released August 5, Muse Code competes directly with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex by coordinating async background agents that stay alive across a full session and logging every action to a crash-safe event log. Meta also open-weighted a 30B distilled version called Muse Glimmer under Apache 2.0 on August 10, hinting that Muse Spark weights could follow.

Alibaba's Qwen3.8 Max reaches general availability as a 2.4 trillion-parameter MoE flagship, with the open Qwen3.8-27B weights released August 14.

The managed API version supports 1M-token context and native multimodal inputs at $2/$6 per million tokens, while open weights (4.9TB in BF16) shipped without vision or full-context support, creating a clear moat for the managed tier. The release adds to a dense cluster of Chinese frontier models competing head-to-head with US labs on both capability and price.
