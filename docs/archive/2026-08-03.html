⚡ Top 5 this week

01
EU AI Act transparency rules go live on August 2 as AI Omnibus pushes high-risk deadlines to late 2027

The European Commission began enforcing Article 50 transparency obligations on August 2, 2026, requiring businesses to disclose AI interactions under penalty of fines up to €15 million. However, the Digital Omnibus on AI — which entered into force July 27 — extended the high-risk system compliance deadline from August 2026 to December 2027, giving enterprises significantly more runway for regulated use cases like recruitment, credit scoring, and critical infrastructure.

02
Open-weight models reach frontier-adjacent quality: Kimi K3 at 2.8 trillion parameters ships with weights promised by July 27

Moonshot AI launched Kimi K3 on July 17 — currently the world's largest open-weight model, with a 2.8-trillion-parameter sparse MoE architecture, native multimodal support, and a 1-million-token context window. Combined with DeepSeek V4 Pro (MIT-licensed, already on Hugging Face) and GLM-5.2, the open-weight tier is now within roughly one benchmark generation of closed frontier leaders like Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.

03
Black Forest Labs launches FLUX 3: a single unified model for image, 20-second video with audio, and factory-floor robotics

On July 23, Black Forest Labs unveiled FLUX 3, jointly trained across image, video, audio, and action-prediction modalities in one architecture — marking the lab's first video model and first robotics play. The FLUX-mimic action variant is already running on Audi production lines handling soft-body manipulation tasks, with video and action modules in gated early access and open weights planned for later in 2026.

04
OpenAI releases GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna) triggering a frontier price war, with Luna priced at just $1 per million input tokens

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 trio — Sol for advanced agentic tasks, Terra for everyday use at half the cost of Sol, and Luna as a high-speed budget option — went to general availability on July 9. Luna's $1/million-input pricing directly undercuts existing volume tiers and forced competitors including Anthropic to remove rate caps and lower introductory prices to retain developer loyalty.

05
EU orders Google to open Android to rival AI assistants and share search data under the Digital Markets Act

The European Commission issued binding requirements forcing Google to allow competing AI assistants onto Android and to share portions of its search index with rival AI developers — affecting access to roughly two billion devices. The ruling came the same week Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly missed its internal quality target for the third consecutive time, compounding competitive pressure on the company.
