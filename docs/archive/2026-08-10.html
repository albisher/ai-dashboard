⚡ Top 5 this week

01
EU AI Act enforcement goes live on August 2, putting chatbot disclosure and deepfake labeling rules into immediate legal effect for all global providers.

The EU AI Office and member-state authorities began active enforcement of Article 50 transparency obligations, requiring AI systems to identify themselves to users and tag synthetic content with machine-readable marks. Non-compliance risks fines of up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, with Anthropic and OpenAI among the highest-profile companies under scrutiny.

02
Alibaba releases Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter open-weight flagship that matches GPT-5.6 on price and claims to trail only Claude Fable 5 in overall capability.

The model went GA on August 3 at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, with open weights for both the flagship and a smaller 27B variant promised around August 10. Alibaba's move signals a shift in the US–China AI race from cost-cutting toward a head-on capability contest for enterprise agent market share.

03
ByteDance ships Seedance 2.5 with 30-second native audio-video generation in a single pass and opens the public developer API on August 7.

The model accepts up to 50 combined reference inputs—images, video clips, and audio clips—and supports timestamp-level editing, a major leap over Seedance 2.0's 9-image, 3-clip ceiling. Chinese video models now hold most top-ten spots in the independent video arena rankings as of August 2026.

04
Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 launches on July 24 as its fourth Claude 5 release in under two months, delivering near-Fable-5 performance at exactly half the price.

Priced at $5 per million input and $25 per million output tokens—same as the outgoing Opus 4.8—the model adds a user-controlled effort toggle (low/medium/high) to help enterprises manage inference costs, and becomes the new default on Claude Max. The rapid release cadence underscores how major labs have shifted from blockbuster launches to continuous iterative shipping.

05
MCP hits a new milestone with its July 28 stateless spec revision and 470M+ monthly SDK downloads, cementing its role as the universal standard for connecting AI agents to external tools.

The fifth major spec update transitions MCP to a fully stateless architecture designed to remove scaling friction in production agentic deployments, with Google Cloud publicly endorsing the new foundation. The protocol, now governed by the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation after Anthropic's December 2025 donation, is spoken natively by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, VS Code, and Cursor.
